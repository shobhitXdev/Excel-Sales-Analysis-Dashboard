# ABC Limited Sales Analysis Dashboard (2021-2025)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project involves a comprehensive analysis of sales data for **ABC Limited** over a 5-year period. The goal was to transform raw sales data into an interactive visual dashboard to help stakeholders track business performance, profitability, and regional growth.

## 📊 Key Features
- **5 KPI Cards:** Real-time tracking of Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Total Quantity, and Average Discount.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic filtering by City, Product Category, Customer Type, and Store Type.
- **Timeline Filter:** Ability to drill down into specific years and months.
- **5 Detailed Visualizations:**
    1. **Sales Trend (Line Chart):** Yearly and monthly growth analysis.
    2. **Category Performance:** Comparison of Sales vs. Profit across categories.
    3. **Store Type Share (Donut Chart):** Distribution between Online and Retail sales.
    4. **Top 10 Cities (Bar Chart):** Identifying high-performing regions.
    5. **Salesperson Leaderboard:** Tracking individual performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Tech Stack
- **Microsoft Excel:** Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and Timelines.
- **Data Cleaning:** Used functions like `TRIM`, `PROPER`, and removal of duplicates to ensure data integrity.
- **Calculated Fields:** Implemented custom formulas within Pivot Tables for Profit Margin % calculations.

## 💡 Key Insights from the Data
- **Profitability:** Identified specific orders (e.g., ORD002) where high discounts led to negative profit margins.
- **Top Performers:** Delhi and Mumbai consistently lead in sales volume.
- **Sales Trend:** Business shows a significant seasonal peak in the last quarter of each year.
- **Segment Analysis:** Premium customers contribute to 40% of the total profit despite being fewer in number.

## 📂 How to Use
1. Clone this repository.
2. Open the `ABC_Limited_Sales_Project.xlsx` file.
3. Use the Slicers on the left and the Timeline at the top to interact with the data.
